# 05 - Target Users# Target Users---# OverviewMedConnect AI is designed as a multi-stakeholder healthcare platform serving patients, healthcare providers, healthcare organizations, and healthcare partners. Each user group has distinct needs, workflows, and permissions.The platform aims to provide a personalized experience for every type of user while maintaining a consistent and intuitive interface.---# Primary Target Users## 1. Patients### DescriptionIndividuals seeking healthcare services, medical consultations, health information, and long-term medical record management.This is the largest user group and the primary focus of the platform.### Age Group18–65+ Years### Digital Literacy- Beginner- Intermediate- Advanced### Common Needs- Check symptoms- Book doctors- Store medical reports- Consult online- Find laboratories- Locate pharmacies- Receive medicine reminders- Track medical history- Learn about diseases### Pain Points- Long waiting times- Lost prescriptions- Difficulty understanding reports- High consultation costs- Multiple healthcare apps- Poor healthcare awareness### Primary Goals- Fast healthcare access- Affordable treatment- Easy report management- Better understanding of health- Convenient consultations---## 2. Doctors### DescriptionRegistered healthcare professionals providing consultations and patient care.### Specializations- General Physician- Cardiologist- Dermatologist- Pediatrician- Orthopedic- Gynecologist- Neurologist- Psychiatrist- Dentist- ENT Specialist### Needs- Appointment management- Patient history- Video consultation- Prescription generation- Medical record access- Follow-up management### Pain Points- Missing patient records- Manual paperwork- Repeated history collection- Appointment scheduling issues### Goals- Better consultation workflow- Faster patient review- Improved patient engagement- Reduced administrative effort---## 3. Hospitals &amp; Clinics### DescriptionHealthcare organizations managing doctors, appointments, and patient services.### Needs- Doctor management- Appointment dashboard- Patient records- Analytics- Consultation tracking### Goals- Better operational efficiency- Improved patient satisfaction- Digital transformation---## 4. Laboratories### DescriptionDiagnostic centers providing medical tests and reports.### Needs- Online bookings- Report uploads- Digital report sharing- Test catalog management### Goals- Increase customer reach- Faster report delivery- Digital workflow---## 5. Pharmacies### DescriptionMedical stores providing medicines and prescription fulfillment.### Needs- Prescription verification- Nearby customer discovery- Medicine catalog- Stock management (future)### Goals- Improve accessibility- Increase visibility- Simplify prescription handling---## 6. Administrators### DescriptionPlatform administrators responsible for operations, moderation, compliance, and analytics.### Responsibilities- User verification- Doctor approval- Content moderation- Partner management- AI monitoring- Platform analytics---# Secondary Target Users## Family MembersFamily members who manage healthcare for:- Children- Parents- Elderly relatives- DependentsFeatures include:- Family profiles- Shared records- Appointment management- Medicine reminders---## CaregiversIndividuals responsible for monitoring elderly or chronically ill patients.---## Healthcare PartnersOrganizations integrating services into the platform.Examples:- Insurance companies- Diagnostic chains- Pharmacy chains- NGOs- Government healthcare programs---# User Segmentation| User Type | Primary Objective | Platform Access ||------------|------------------|----------------|| Patient | Healthcare Management | Patient Portal || Doctor | Patient Care | Doctor Portal || Hospital | Operations | Hospital Dashboard || Laboratory | Diagnostics | Lab Dashboard || Pharmacy | Medicine Services | Pharmacy Dashboard || Admin | Platform Management | Admin Panel |---# Priority Matrix### Highest Priority- Patients- Doctors### Medium Priority- Laboratories- Pharmacies### Future Expansion- Insurance Providers- Government Health Systems- Corporate Wellness Programs- Wearable Device Users---
